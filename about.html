About Us

Our Story & Mission

Founded in 2017, KKP Global exists to call men back to prayer, restoring their identity as kings and priests in Christ.

👑 Who We Are

King, Kings and Priests Global was founded on 7 November 2017 out of a burden to call men back to the place of prayer with eternity in view. What began quietly has grown through consistency, discipline, and conviction.

📖 Our Foundation

"And hast made us unto our God kings and priests..." — Revelation 5:10

Men are not called to live without direction. In Christ, they are restored to responsibility, authority, and priesthood. This is the theological foundation of KKP Global.

🎯 Our Motto

Reconciling men to Christ through prayer.

This simple yet powerful statement captures our core mission: bringing men back into relationship with Jesus through the transformative power of prayer.

🔭 Our Vision

👨 Manhood

Restoring identity and responsibility in Christ

👨‍👩‍👦 Fatherhood

Strengthening leadership in families

🤝 Brotherhood

Building accountability and community

Men united in prayer, standing as kings and priests before God.
